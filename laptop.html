Info & Guidelines

School software compatibility, longevity advice, and operating system overviews.

School Compatibility

Supported by school: macOS and Windows. Don't get Chromebooks, Googlebooks or other operating systems. Everything on this list is supported.

Both operating systems can do everything needed for school.

To get a laptop that lasts from Year 7 to around Year 11–12, avoid Cheap and maybe Low Tier.

macOS

macOS is Apple's Unix-based operating system designed exclusively for Mac computers. It is easier to use, highly optimized, works seamlessly with Apple devices, and is best for creative workflows.

Windows

Windows is Microsoft's operating system designed for unmatched hardware flexibility and software compatibility. It is best for office work, enterprise tools, and broad PC gaming support.
